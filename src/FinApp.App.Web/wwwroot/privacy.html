Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

TandemTab ("we", "us") is a personal budgeting app that helps you track spending, budgets and savings. All the data in the app is what you choose to enter or import yourself — for example from a bank statement file (CSV, Excel, OFX, QIF or XML). What you import, and whether you import anything at all, is entirely up to you. This policy explains what personal data we process and why. It is written to comply with the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Who we are

TandemTab is operated by TandemTab Company, Sofia, Bulgaria. For any privacy question or to exercise your rights, contact us at admin@tandemtab.com.

What we collect

  • Account details — the email address and username you register with, or the name, email and profile picture your sign-in provider (Google/Facebook) shares if you sign in that way.
  • Budgeting data you enter — accounts, categories, budgets, expenses, savings and contributions you create in the app.
  • Files you import — if you import a bank statement file, we process the transactions it contains (dates, amounts, descriptions) to create budgeting entries you review. Importing is optional and entirely under your control; the file is processed to build those entries and isn't stored separately.
  • Diagnostic / error reports — if the app hits an error we record a technical report (the error message and code location, the app version and browser type, and your account id if you are signed in) so we can find and fix it. These reports are automatically stripped of financial details — amounts, names and the like are redacted before the report leaves your device — and they are used only to keep the app working. This is diagnostic information, not your budgeting data.
  • Feedback you send — if you use a feedback form, we store your rating and comment so we can act on it. A comment is only ever shown publicly if you tick the box asking us to; otherwise it stays private to us.

Importing statements

Instead of connecting a bank, you can import a statement file you export from your bank yourself. The app reads the transactions locally to prepare draft entries; nothing becomes a budgeting entry until you review and confirm it. You decide what to import and what to leave out.

The assistant

TandemTab has an optional assistant that answers questions about your money and takes you to the right screen. It is off unless you turn it on, and turning it on is a separate, explicit consent you can withdraw at any time by not using it.

It is not switched on for most accounts yet. While we are still testing it, the assistant is available to a small number of accounts; on every other account it simply does not appear, and nothing described in this section happens. We are telling you how it works now rather than when it reaches you.

Most questions never leave your device. The app already holds your account, so it answers what it can locally. When a question needs interpreting, we send it to Anthropic (model claude-haiku-4-5) — but not as you typed it.

Your words are taken out first, on your device. The names of your categories, goals, wallets and journeys are replaced with numbered placeholders, and every number is removed. A question like “how is my Car fund doing after the 250 I put in at Lidl” leaves as “how is my {1} doing after the ### I put in at Lidl”, with a separate note saying only that {1} is a goal. If a word looks personal and the app does not recognise it, the question is not sent at all — the assistant tells you so instead of guessing.

What this means in practice: no balance, amount, category, goal, wallet or journey name is ever sent, and no part of your account, its history or its documents is sent. Only the reshaped sentence above and the list of placeholder types go. Each question is sent on its own — no earlier questions, answers or conversation history travel with it — and we do not store the question ourselves.

Any figure the assistant reads back to you was worked out on your own device from your own data. The model is never given a number and never returns one, so it cannot get one wrong.

Where it goes. Anthropic is based in the United States, so this step involves a transfer outside the EU. It is limited to the reshaped question described above. Anthropic processes it to produce the answer and handles it under its own terms as our processor; we do not authorise it to be used to train models. If you would rather nothing left your device at all, leave the assistant off — everything else in TandemTab works exactly the same.

Shared accounts

A TandemTab account can be shared: the owner may invite other people, who then become members and can see and edit that account's contents. The owner can remove a member, transfer ownership, or leave the account at any time; a removed member immediately loses access. A shared account may contain another person's financial information, so we recommend members keep its contents confidential and enable two-factor authentication.

Why we process it (legal bases)

  • Performance of a contract — to provide the budgeting service you sign up for.
  • Your consent — for optional features such as signing in with Google or Facebook, or turning on the assistant, which you can withdraw at any time.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers who help us run TandemTab:

  • Google Cloud — hosting and database, in the EU (europe-west1 region).
  • Google / Facebook — only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • Anthropic (United States) — only if you turn the assistant on, and only the reshaped question described under The assistant: no names, no amounts, no account data.

Storage, security and retention

Your data is hosted in the European Union, transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections, and encrypted at rest using managed encryption keys (Google Cloud KMS). Access credentials are held server-side and protected. We keep your data for as long as your account exists. If you leave or delete an account it is archived and permanently deleted after 30 days. You can delete your data at any time by removing your accounts. Diagnostic / error reports live in our server logs and are kept only for a short period (typically up to 30 days) before rolling off automatically.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to, correction or deletion of your data, restrict or object to processing, request portability, and withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any right, email admin@tandemtab.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection (CPDP) or your local authority.

Cookies

We use only the storage strictly necessary to keep you signed in and remember preferences (such as your theme and language). We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

Changes
